# Team Rituals

- Standup stays under fifteen minutes and focuses on blockers, not a
  status readout of everything everyone did — a detailed status update
  belongs in a written form people can read on their own time, not in a
  meeting where everyone else is waiting for their turn to speak.
- Retrospectives produce at most three action items per cycle, each with
  an owner; a retro that generates twelve good ideas and ships none of
  them because nobody owned any of them is worse for morale than a retro
  that honestly produced only one small fix that actually happened.
- Planning estimates are a shared team activity, not a number one person
  hands down — the people doing the work have information about the
  actual complexity that the person writing the ticket usually doesn't,
  and skipping their input produces estimates that are confidently wrong.
